## Video direction

Use the existing Chusky palette and tokens in `frame.md`: white/near-white surfaces, ink-black controls and type, and neutral gray hairlines. Use Instrument Sans for interface/body, Instrument Serif for selected display phrases, and JetBrains Mono only for compact metadata. Keep content inside the top 83% for the caption-safe band. One fine black progress rule is the continuous visual thread at the same lower-content alignment and scale in every frame; it advances through research, saved work, approval, conversation, scheduled actions, and the mission receipt, then resolves on the end card. Match cuts align that rule and the mission heading across surfaces. Use quiet mask reveals, restrained element fades/rises, and occasional single cursor presses. Motion follows long-tail ease and sequential VO-paced reveals; do not front-load. Allocate still holds to the end of research, each approved action state, the later timeline, the summary, and the CTA. Keep the feel editorial and calm; no glow, decorative AI motifs, purple/blue gradients, or mascot illustrations beyond the supplied wordmark. Do not turn the film into a slideshow of fully formed cards or a screensaver of independently floating UI. All composed product surfaces are visibly marked `ILLUSTRATIVE WORKFLOW`.
