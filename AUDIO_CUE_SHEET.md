# Audio cue sheet

## Included audio

Narration is generated locally with Kokoro ONNX using the `am_michael` American English voice at speed 0.96. Separate WAV clips are aligned to each storyboard beat in `assets/voice/`; equivalent clips are included in the portrait project and 15-second cutdown. The voice is synthetic, not a human recording.

The music bed is “Meanwhile” by Scott Buckley, downloaded from the composer’s official library and released under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. It is trimmed to the video durations, faded in and out, lowered under narration, and voiceover-carved for clarity. The finished video is a new audiovisual work; the track is not distributed separately. See `CREDITS.txt` for attribution and publishing guidance. [Track and attribution](https://www.scottbuckley.com.au/library/meanwhile/) · [CC BY 4.0 license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

No sound effects are included. Optional future cues below are not used in the current mix. Any additions must be supplied or appropriately licensed.
