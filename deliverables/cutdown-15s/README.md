# Chusky 15-second launch cutdown

Editable HyperFrames composition: 1920×1080, 30 fps, 15 seconds. Open `index.html` as the project entry point; `hyperframes.json` and `package.json` are included for the local HyperFrames workflow.

The cutdown follows one illustrative mission through a request, research and shortlist, a visible approval gate and scheduled next step, calls and meeting participation, a clear Day 1 → Day 2 → Later time jump, and a concise mission summary with a pilot CTA. Interface content is illustrative. Prospect labels are generic; no outcomes are guaranteed.

- `narration.txt` is a clean-text VO guide. It has not been recorded or generated.
- `captions.srt` follows the 15-second edit as a timing guide. Retiming may be needed to match a recorded read.
- No music or sound effects are included. There were no supplied or licensed audio assets.
- `assets/` contains the supplied Chusky wordmark and local brand font files copied into this isolated project.

The composition uses one paused GSAP timeline registered to the root composition ID, timed scene clips, and seek-safe authored motion. Scene transitions are clean cuts matched on a persistent progress rule and consistent editorial layout.
